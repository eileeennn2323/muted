01 — the problem

Today, we take meeting notes and jot down observations about people and the dynamics

— but those insights stay buried. We never turn them into practical guidance.

  • Valuable observations and lessons learnt are scattered across meeting notes.
  • We remember what happened, but not what it means.
  • We struggle to turn repeated interactions into practical strategies for working with people.
an actual board — blurred for privacy
02 — the idea

Imagine opening the app before a meeting and instantly seeing:

  • What this person likely cares about
  • Questions they'll probably ask
  • How to communicate effectively with them
  • What to avoid saying
  • Leadership behaviours worth learning (or avoiding)

You feed it notes and it builds a living playbook for every person you work with, seen only by you.

03 — meet the product

Muted.

A private, AI-powered relationship intelligence assistant that helps you learn from the people around you, predict how conversations will go, and build the right strategy for working with them.

Your second brain for people at work.

04 — the 3 outcomes

Learn. Predict. Strategise.

Learn

What can I learn from this person?

e.g. "this director asks sharp questions like ___," "this BA is great at managing stakeholders, here's what he would say:___."

This becomes my personal library of leadership and communication lessons.

Predict

If I need to interact with them next week, what should I expect?

e.g. "they hate surprises," "they need at least 24 hours heads-up before making decisions."

I'm no longer reacting — I can anticipate.

Strategise

Not "this person is difficult" — but "given what I know, what's the best way to communicate with them?"

e.g. "don't spend 10 minutes on background — jump straight to the recommendation.", "don't use big words on this person"

Tailor communication to each person: know what works, what to avoid, and how to build stronger relationships.
